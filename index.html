Butler: a private family assistant

Butler is a private, non-commercial assistant used by a single household. It is not offered to the public, and you cannot sign up for it.

Privacy policy

  • Who uses it: only members of one family and the family's own test accounts.
  • What it accesses: with each account holder's explicit consent through Google sign-in, Butler may read and manage that account's Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts. It acts only when a family member asks it to.
  • Where data goes: Google data is processed by the family's own self-hosted server and the AI services that server is configured to use. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train general-purpose AI models.
  • Storage: sign-in tokens and any saved notes stay on the family's private server. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Google API Services: Butler's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Contact: the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
